# E05/E17 provisional assumptions and safeguards

The synthetic journey uses Uzbekistan, established incorporated SMEs, food processing, cold-storage/
logistics and light manufacturing, and commercial solar PV under an illustrative Ijarah structure.
The USD 25,000–250,000 range and normal two-year operating history are versioned guidance, not
hard-coded approval rules or statutory definitions.

Start-ups, unusual structures, prohibited activities, sanctions/PEP concerns, ownership discrepancies
and insolvency indicators route to accountable human review. They are not automatically approved or
rejected. All fixture identities, references, premises, financials, owners and documents are fictional.

Out of scope: binding underwriting rules, legal/regulatory/Shariah certification, autonomous decisions,
live KYC/AML, real money, settlement, custody, production register/transfer-agent action, token transfer
and public deployment.
